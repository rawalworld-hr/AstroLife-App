# Rawalworld Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 23 August 2026**

Rawalworld provides lifestyle, astrology, travel, shopping, booking, news, weather, entertainment and related service features. This Privacy Policy explains the information Rawalworld may collect, how it is used and the choices available to users.

## Information users provide
Rawalworld may collect information that users voluntarily enter when they use booking, shopping, profile or service-request features. This may include name, mobile number, email address, city, delivery address, pincode, preferred date, service requirements, birth details submitted for astrology services, product order information and other information supplied in a request.

## How information is used
Information is used to provide requested services, manage bookings and orders, respond to enquiries, arrange delivery or service follow-up, maintain customer records, manage the Rawalworld service catalogue and improve the operation of the app.

## Admin accounts
The Rawalworld Admin area uses authenticated access. Admin credentials and authentication are handled through the connected Supabase authentication service. Authorized administrators can manage products, categories/masters, gallery content, service contacts, payment settings, bookings, orders and client records.

## Payments
Rawalworld may display or use a UPI / Google Pay payment identifier configured by an authorized administrator. When a user chooses to pay, the app can hand payment information such as payee, amount and order note to an installed UPI-compatible application. Rawalworld does not ask users to enter or store UPI PINs, bank passwords or card credentials inside the Rawalworld app.

## Third-party websites and services
Some Rawalworld features open external websites or apps for maps, flight and train information, hotel search, weather, news, astrology resources, entertainment, cricket, games, music and payments. These external services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Rawalworld is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services.

## Storage and backend services
Rawalworld uses a connected Supabase backend for selected app records and authentication. Information submitted through online booking, order, product, gallery, service-contact and administration functions may be stored in that backend subject to its configured access controls and retention practices.

## Data sharing
Rawalworld does not sell users' personal information. Information may be accessible to authorized Rawalworld administrators and service personnel when needed to fulfil a booking, order, enquiry or other requested service. Information may also be processed by technical service providers used to operate the app and backend.

## Data retention
Information may be retained for as long as needed to provide services, maintain business and transaction records, meet legal or operational requirements and resolve disputes. Records may be deleted or anonymized when they are no longer needed, subject to applicable requirements.

## Security
Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information. No online system can guarantee absolute security, and users should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive information through free-text fields.

## Children's privacy
Rawalworld is not specifically designed for children under 13. If personal information from a child is identified and is not required for a legitimate service requested by a parent or guardian, reasonable steps may be taken to remove it.

## User choices and requests
Users may contact Rawalworld to ask about personal information associated with their bookings, orders or service requests and to request correction or deletion where appropriate and legally permitted.

## Changes to this policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated as Rawalworld features, service providers or legal requirements change. The effective date will be updated when material changes are made.
